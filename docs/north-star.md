# FliLaunch North Star

## The guiding idea

**Get from transcript to launch-ready in one focused session.**

The creator should never have to hold the whole video in their head while packaging it.
The tool holds it for them.

---

## What this means in practice

- The transcript is the memory. Everything else derives from it.
- The tool does the forgetting-prevention work, not the creator.
- Every stage output should be immediately usable — not a starting point for more work.
- Fast is a feature. Friction is the enemy.

---

## What this is NOT

- Not a channel strategy tool
- Not a content calendar
- Not an analytics platform
- Not a recommendation engine
- Not a brand intelligence system

Those may come later. The North Star for v1 is narrow and sharp:
**transcript → packaged and ready to publish**.

---

## The test

If a new feature helps the creator get from transcript to launch faster or more consistently — it fits.
If it requires the creator to do more thinking, enter more data, or manage more systems — it doesn't.

---

## Memorable framing

> "The video is done. Now the tool does the launch work."
